# Chinese tutor session: 2026-10-27

Level: beginner, A1, Immersive Chinese lesson 80. Topic: when the internship ends, working at Nidec Aerospace or Nidec's office in Qingdao, how far you get from Qingdao in an hour, flying (never flown), going to China with the girlfriend.

## Patterns to watch

| | |
|---|---|
| Wrong word from the pinyin keyboard (7 this session) | 原→**远** (twice), 主→**住**, 神怎→**深圳**, 做→**坐** (飞机), 群岛→**青岛**, 他→**她** (girlfriend) |
| Place before the verb, with 在 | 我想工作Nidec Aerospace → 我想**在**Nidec Aerospace工作; 我会住群岛 → 我会住**在**青岛 |
| 在 as the main verb ("is in") goes missing | 那我不我的城市 → 但是它不**在**我的城市 |
| Dates: year before month, time before the verb | 开始了1月2026年 → **2026年1月**开始 |
| An adjective before a noun needs 是 and 的 | 都很漂亮城市 → 它们都**是**很漂亮**的**城市 |
| "Far from" needs 离 | 住工作的原 → 住在**离**公司很远的地方 |
| English words as gaps (3) | Aerospace (a name), EU, "don't always have reasons" |
| Works now | 我不知道; 对,在青岛; 我没坐过飞机 (过, from the frame); 北京和上海都想去; 中国的Nidec办公室也很好 |

## Gap words (English the learner used)

EU → 欧洲 Ōuzhōu (Europe; 欧盟 Ōuméng is the EU itself); don't always have reasons → 不一定有原因 bù yídìng yǒu yuányīn. Given in my answers: 高铁 gāotiě (high-speed train), 海边 hǎibiān (by the sea). Aerospace stays in English (part of a company name).

## Tutor notes

- I asked 你在家一般做什么? although `Memory.md` already answers it (cooking, calls with the girlfriend, MLBB, the mod, films and games). The learner called it out. Check `Memory.md` before every question, not just the opening one.
- The learner said people don't always have reasons: stop putting 因为 in every 💡 frame.
- I said the problem was also the topic switch; the learner disagreed: people switch topics naturally. Switching is fine; asking what's already known is not.
- My slips: read 痘院 as 道院 (it was 多远 by sound); called 什么…吗 acceptable ("any city at all?") where the learner likely meant "which cities".
- Prices I gave (Qingdao–Beijing 500–1000 yuan by plane, 3+ hours by high-speed train) are rough.
- I opened with the question left over from the last session; the learner asked that sessions never start from an old
  conversation. Also: they asked to be able to ask questions too, not only answer them, and for sessions to cover
  beginner grammar and words that haven't come up yet. `Tutor.md` reworked for all three; `Grammar.md` added.
