# Chinese diary: October 2026

Written by the learner, before the tutor sessions started. Copied as written. The learner may have looked words up
while writing, so the words here are not counted as known and are not in `Vocab.md`.

## 13日

我去看我的女朋友。星期五下班以后，我回家，拿了我的东西，然后去汽车站了。

我我坐车去了 Volta Redonda。一个小时以后，我在汽车站坐公交车去 Rio de Janeiro，然后又换了一辆公交车去 Cabo Frio。

## Notes for the tutor

- Natural throughout: 下班以后, 然后, 又换了一辆公交车. A good model of time order for longer sentences.
