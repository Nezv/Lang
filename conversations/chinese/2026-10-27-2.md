# Chinese tutor session: 2026-10-27 (second)

Level: beginner, A1, Immersive Chinese lesson 80. Topic: the weather now in the learner's city, the coldest winter
day, a favourite temperature, never having seen snow, Harbin, Shenzhen's and Beijing's weather (the learner no longer
wants to live in Shenzhen), days until summer, Christmas in summer. Then a photo of a 10-colour ballpoint pen and what
a barcode tells you.

Grammar point: 呢 (你的城市呢? 北京呢?). The learner wrote 深圳呢? after seeing it in my rows and frames.

Unseen words brought in: 天气 (Anki L71), 冷 (Anki L72), 下雨 (Anki L71). The learner wrote 冷 and 下雨.

## Patterns to watch

| | |
|---|---|
| Wrong word from the pinyin keyboard (3) | 主→**住** (again), 听→**挺** (again), 无毒→**五度** |
| English words as gaps (4) | summer (夏天 is a 🌿 word), agree, sounds, use winter clothes |
| 多什么 for 多少 and for 什么意思 | 冬天和summer是多什么? → 多少度?; 几乎有意思多什么? → 几乎是什么意思? |
| Times of day | 早的6点 → 早上六点; 早的12点 → 中午十二点 |
| Numbers | 一十三°C → 十三度 |
| 住 then 在 + place | 在主深圳 → 住在深圳 |
| 太 without 了 | 太热 → 太热了 |
| Works now | 我没见过雪 (过, from the frame); 深圳呢?; 最冷6度; 我最喜欢16度; 我觉得还有六十天左右 (左右 right after I gave it) |

Natural as written: 我没见过雪; 深圳呢?; 最冷6度; 我最喜欢16度; 我觉得还有六十天左右.

## Gap words (English the learner used)

summer → 夏天 xiàtiān; agree → 同意 tóngyì; sounds → 听起来 tīng qǐlai; use (clothes) → 穿 chuān; winter clothes → 冬天的衣服
dōngtiān de yīfu. Asked about: 几乎 jīhū (almost), 圆珠笔 yuánzhūbǐ (ballpoint pen). Given in my rows: 度 dù (degrees),
雪 xuě (snow), 零下 língxià (below zero), 左右 zuǒyòu (about), 舒服 shūfu (comfortable), 春天 chūntiān, 秋天 qiūtiān,
圣诞节 Shèngdàn jié (Christmas).

## Tutor notes

- The learner's idea: in sessions on other topics, a quick quiz question such as 离夏天还有多少天? brings back the
  season words without another weather conversation. Added to `Tutor.md`.
- I used 几乎 without a gloss and the learner had to ask: gloss new words in the row. Several turns brought 2–3 new
  words at once (舒服, 左右, 春天, 秋天).
- A 💡 frame assumed an opinion (我不喜欢冷的天气); the learner likes cold weather. Frames can offer both sides.
- My slip: I said a barcode starting with 69 means made in China. It only means the company registered its barcode
  in China. Corrected in the next turn.
