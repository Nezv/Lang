# Chinese diary: September 2026

Written by the learner, before the tutor sessions started. Copied as written. The learner may have looked words up
while writing, so the words here are not counted as known and are not in `Vocab.md`.

## 1日

我早上五小时wake up，一个小时后六造风车，七点到上班，但是上班时期天半，所以中文学了半个小时。办公室的老板也在上班。

## 11日

## 12日

我很困。已经在公交车上。我星期六和星期天很好玩儿，电影看了，玩了游戏。所以上班真没意思。

## 16日

## Notes for the tutor

- Typing slips: 在 for 再 (15日, 不想让你再做这个了); probably 六点坐公车 for 六造风车 (1日, my guess).
- 1日: 五小时 for 五点 (o'clock), and "wake up" in English (起床).
- 11日: 至家 for 到家; 需要站了 for 得站着 (had to stand).
- 16日: "whether" is A没A: 警察来没来我不知道, like 有没有 in `Vocab.md`.
- Unclear, left as written: 上班时期天半 (1日), 我能睡了 (16日, maybe 我正要睡).
- The 14日 and 15日 lines read like practice sentences, so `Memory.md` takes only the plan to live together from them.
