## About the learner

### Who they are

- Brazilian, 25 (diary, 2026-08). Has always liked learning languages.
- Wants a Chinese name (diary, 2026-08).
- No Chinese person has heard their Chinese yet (diary, 2026-08).

### Daily life

- Drinks tea at the office (the office sells fruit teas; likes apple flavour) and coffee at home.
- Makes coffee with 200 ml milk, 1 tbsp instant coffee and 1 tbsp sugar, no water; prefers it hot, even in summer.
- Often forgets to buy milk.
- Drinks two Monster Mango Loco a day, up to about 7 pm; sleeps fine.
- Goes to bed at 10 pm on weekdays and at midnight on weekends.
- Cooks when they get home, then chats with their girlfriend on the phone (diary, 2026-08).
- Once called the police, for the first time ever, when a young man parked in front of the house and played loud
  music; it stopped after 20 minutes (diary, 2026-09-16).

### Study and work

- University student; does an internship at Nidec. Said on 2026-10-27 that it started in January 2026 and ends in
  June 2027 (about eight months left). In August went from 6 to 8 hours a day; works at a computer in the office.
- Doesn't know yet whether to stay at Nidec after the internship. Would like to work at Nidec Aerospace, but it isn't
  in their city; Nidec's office in China, in Qingdao, would be good too. Doesn't like living far from work, so in
  China would live in Qingdao.
- In August wrote that the job is a bit hard and tiring but teaches something every day, and that the colleagues are
  already good friends who chat a lot; on 2026-10-21 called the colleagues "OK". Eats lunch with them at 11:00 in the
  company restaurant.
- Sometimes sees Chinese people at work and sees a lot of Chinese because of the job; some colleagues have been to
  China (diary, 2026-08).
- Studies Chinese on the bus to work (Immersive Chinese, and Claude Code on the phone); in August studied about two
  hours a day, before and after work.
- Programmed a Chinese app (Android and Windows) with AI and finds AI programming fun; dropped it on 2026-10-25 and
  uses the Claude app for tutoring instead. Makes apps, mods, documents and 3D models with AI; 3D models are the
  favourite.
- Uses Claude on the phone any time: on the bus, at lunch, after cooking. The computer stays at home and the phone
  reaches it remotely (Claude Code). Says they don't really program, they ask Claude.

### Family and home

- Family of four: dad, mum, a younger brother (10, still in school) and the learner. The brother's dad is not the
  learner's dad; they aren't very close and rarely see each other.
- Still lives with their mum.
- Away from home from 5 am to 7 pm; the bus to the office takes 90 minutes each way (the office is far). In August
  the diary gave half an hour to an hour.
- Can't do much about the distance; plans to buy their own house near the office when the internship ends.

### Girlfriend

- A long-distance relationship. They plan to live together in about a year; they are waiting because it needs a lot

- Visited her in October 2026: after work on a Friday, a bus to Volta Redonda, then to Rio de Janeiro, then another
  to Cabo Frio (diary, 2026-10-13).
- Plays MLBB (Mobile Legends) with her almost every day. Favourite hero: Beatrix (ビアトリクス).

### Free time

- Makes burgers about twice a week and eats them alone: 200 g beef, cheddar, bacon, soy-sauce onions.
- Likes Anna German and Frank Sinatra; listens to music less since dating and working.
- Makes a Minecraft mod with 3D models: 40 dinosaur models, item models and a tech (progression) tree. Favourite

- Weekends: films and games (diary, 2026-09).

### Japan and China

- Studied Japanese for many years at school, self-taught (they only ever had an English teacher), and stopped at
  university. Wanted to go to Japan back then and to live there. The reason is not anime (they found that guess
  offensive): they have liked Japanese history, architecture and universities since long ago. Wanted to study at
  Kyushu University (九大).
- Now wants to live and work in Shenzhen instead, a big city that looks like cyberpunk (2026-10-26).
- Is about to finish university in Brazil (2026-10-26; unclear whether done or nearly done).
- Still likes Japan, but has no money to go now. Wants to learn both Japanese and Chinese.
- In Tokyo, would eat anything at the first place they walk into, have drinks and walk around town.
- Has only had Japanese food in Brazil; doesn't eat Brazilian yakisoba much.
- Didn't plan to learn Chinese at first, then came to like China too. Wants to go, but it's too expensive for now
  (diary, 2026-08).
- Has never been on a plane. Would most like to fly to China, Japan or Europe, with no particular reason ("people
  don't always have reasons"). Wants to see both Beijing and Shanghai. Would go to China with their girlfriend.
