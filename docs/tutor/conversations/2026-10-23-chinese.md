# Chinese tutor session: 2026-10-23

Level: beginner, Immersive Chinese lesson 80. Topic: family (younger brother), living with mum, the 90-minute bus commute, buying a house near the office after the internship. Restarted once at the start: the planned opener (3D models) was from an old conversation, so the session opened on family instead.

## Patterns to watch

| | |
|---|---|
| Wrong character from the pinyin keyboard | 原→**远** (yuǎn) |
| Verb order: the verb comes before the person it acts on | 我一般他不看 → 我一般**看不见他** |
| A measure phrase needs a verb | 下班也九十分钟 → 下班也**要**九十分钟 |
| Family words: 哥哥 is the older brother | 哥哥 (younger brother) → **弟弟** |
| Time before place before verb | 结束后我会买…家,公司的附近 → 结束以后,我会**在公司附近买**房子 |
| Literal word for word from English breaks the sentence | 做东西不在 ("can't do anything"), 买机子的家 ("buy my own house") |
| English words as gaps (much fewer than before: only 3 this session) | internship, 5AM-7PM, "younger brother" |
| Longer sentences work now: 因为…所以 and 对,…,… held together | 他的爸爸不是我的爸爸,所以我和他是原 |

## My sentences and their corrections

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ | 我家有四个人,妈妈,爸爸,哥哥 (younger brother) |
| ✅ | 我家有四个人:爸爸、妈妈、弟弟和我。<br>Wǒ jiā yǒu sì ge rén: bàba, māma, dìdi hé wǒ.<br>There are four people in my family: dad, mum, my younger brother and me. |
| ⛔ | 不常常和他一起玩儿 |
| ✅ | 我不常和他一起玩儿。<br>Wǒ bù cháng hé tā yìqǐ wánr.<br>I don't often play with him. (不常常 is understood; 不常 is more common) |
| ⛔ | 我没有时间,5AM-7PM工作 (from when I wake up till I get home) |
| ✅ | 我从早上五点到晚上七点都不在家。<br>Wǒ cóng zǎoshang wǔ diǎn dào wǎnshang qī diǎn dōu bú zài jiā.<br>From 5 am to 7 pm I'm not at home. |
| ⛔ | 我一般他不看 |
| ✅ | 我一般看不见他。<br>Wǒ yìbān kàn bu jiàn tā.<br>I usually don't see him. |
| ⛔ | 所以我和他是原 (typing slip) |
| ✅ | 所以我和他不太近。(my best guess, the learner confirmed)<br>Suǒyǐ wǒ hé tā bú tài jìn.<br>So he and I aren't very close. |
| ⛔ | 下班也九十分钟 |
| ✅ | 我上班要九十分钟,下班也要九十分钟。<br>Wǒ shàngbān yào jiǔshí fēnzhōng, xiàbān yě yào jiǔshí fēnzhōng.<br>It takes me 90 minutes to get to work and 90 minutes to get home. |
| ⛔ | 太长了,做东西不在,因为工作室internship,结束后我会买机子的家,公司的附近 (misread by me; it meant "It's too far, I can't do anything about it, my job is an internship, when it ends I'll buy my own house near the office") |
| ✅ | 太远了,我没有办法。我的工作是实习,结束以后,我会在公司附近买自己的房子。<br>Tài yuǎn le, wǒ méiyǒu bànfǎ. Wǒ de gōngzuò shì shíxí, jiéshù yǐhòu, wǒ huì zài gōngsī fùjìn mǎi zìjǐ de fángzi.<br>It's too far, there's nothing I can do. My job is an internship; after it ends, I'll buy my own house near the office. |

Natural as written: 我家有四个人。对,他还在上学,他十岁。他的爸爸不是我的爸爸。我还和妈妈住在一起。对,公司很远。我上班需要九十分钟,很远。

## Gap words (English the learner used)

younger brother → 弟弟 dìdi; internship → 实习 shíxí (again); 5AM-7PM → 从早上五点到晚上七点. Asked: 9 → 九 jiǔ. Given in corrections: 房子 fángzi (house), 办法 bànfǎ (way, option), 看不见 kàn bu jiàn (can't see).
