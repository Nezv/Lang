# Vocabulary

Words from the tutor conversations, with how well the learner knows them. The tutor works 🌱 and 🌿 words back into
its questions and moves them up as the learner uses them.

The Immersive Chinese word list (lessons 1–79) stays in Anki, in `../Anki/practice/vocab.md`: this repo is public and
the list is course content. Anki tracks the maturity of those words itself. "Anki L44" below marks a course word that
came up in a conversation.

## Maturity

| | Level | Meaning | Moves up when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌱 | Seen | The tutor gave it: the learner wrote English, or it came in a correction | The learner writes it |
| 🌿 | Used | The learner has written it at least once (a typing slip still counts) | It's used right, without help, in a second session |
| 🌳 | Known | Used right, without help, in two sessions | — |

A word drops back to 🌱 when the learner needs it given again.
